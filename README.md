### 👋 About Me

- 🔭 Part of **Centauri Stars** (Centaurus High School, Lafayette, CO) — **1 of 5 winning teams worldwide**, out of 712 international applicants, in **CERN's 2026 Beamline for Schools** competition
- ⚛️ Helped build a working **SiPM-based DIRC Cherenkov detector** from scratch — wrote the Python photon-transport simulation, designed the PCB and mechanical mounts, and ran two weeks of beamtime at **DESY, Hamburg**
- 🔬 Deep in analog signal-chain design for a **CosmicWatch cosmic-ray muon detector** — SiPM biasing, amplification, peak detection, and a custom 3.3V→40V boost converter
- ⌨️ Currently designing a custom mechanical **keyboard PCB with a built-in OLED screen** around an ESP32
- 🎨 Also work across software, PCB/CAD engineering, and digital art — full portfolio at [ryanlgriffith.com](https://ryanlgriffith.com)
